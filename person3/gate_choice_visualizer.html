The rules, in plain language

Aircraft and gate type — hard ruleCargo goes only to cargo gates. International passengers need international gates. Domestic passengers can use domestic or international gates.
Wingspan and jetbridge — hard rulesThe gate's wingspan limit must fit the aircraft. If the flight needs a jetbridge, the gate must have one.
Bookings and outages — hard rulesOverlapping intervals conflict. Intervals are half-open, so one flight ending at 11:00 and another starting at 11:00 may share a gate.
Walking — soft costEach metre adds 0.01 points. A shorter walk is better, once safety rules are satisfied.
Customs gate use — soft costA domestic flight at an international gate adds 5 points, leaving more customs capacity for international flights.
Moving a flight — soft costKeeping its current gate costs 0. Moving it costs 10 points before arrival or 50 after arrival, when a current time is supplied.
